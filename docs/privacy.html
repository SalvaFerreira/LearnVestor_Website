Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

LearnVestor is free and does not require an account. The browser demo runs entirely inside your browser: your demo profile and progress are stored only in your browser's storage and are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data erases them.

The app collects the following types of information:

  • No Account Data: We do not ask for your name, email address or password. The app creates a random, anonymous identifier for your device.
  • Profile Information: The username you choose (shown on the public weekly leaderboard), age bracket, country and language from your device settings, time zone and daily goal
  • Learning Progress: Lesson completion, quiz scores, and achievement data
  • Portfolio Data: Virtual trading activities and investment preferences
  • Usage Analytics: App usage patterns and feature interactions, only if you opt in
  • Waitlist: If you join the waitlist on this website, your email address, used only to tell you when the app launches
  • No Payment Data: The app has no subscriptions or purchases, so we never collect payment information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our educational services
  • Track your learning progress and achievements
  • Personalize your learning experience
  • Tell waitlist members when the app launches
  • Analyze app usage to improve features

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and prevent fraud
  • With service providers who help us operate the app (under strict confidentiality)

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Secure hosting with Supabase and industry-standard practices

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your progress and data at any time in the app (Profile > Settings)
  • Export your learning progress data
  • Leave the waitlist at any time

6. Third-Party Services

LearnVestor integrates with:

  • Finnhub API: For real-time stock price data
  • Supabase: For secure data storage and the anonymous device identifier

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services. Learning progress and profile data are kept while you use the app. Because there is no account, uninstalling the app or clearing its data means you can no longer access your progress, and profiles that stay inactive for a long time may be deleted. Anonymized analytics data may be retained longer for service improvement.

8. Children's Privacy

LearnVestor is intended for users 16 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes through the app or on this website. Your continued use of LearnVestor after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@learnvestor.app
  • Support: support@learnvestor.app
