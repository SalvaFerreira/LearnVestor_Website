Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Who We Are

LearnVestor is a personal, non-commercial project by Salvador Ferreira, based in Portugal, who is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. For anything about your data, write to salvador.bras.ferreira@gmail.com.

This policy covers this website and the free browser demo. The future mobile app will have its own privacy policy.

2. The Browser Demo

The demo does not have accounts and does not ask for your name, email or password. It runs entirely inside your browser:

  • Your demo profile, lesson progress, achievements and virtual portfolio are stored only in your browser's storage on your device. They are never sent to us and we cannot see them.
  • The demo reads your browser's language, country and time zone to set up the lessons and the virtual currency. These also stay in your browser.
  • You can erase everything with the "Reset progress" button in the demo or by clearing your browser data for this site.

3. Waitlist and Feedback

We only receive personal data if you choose to send it through the forms on this website:

  • Waitlist: your email address, used only to tell you when the LearnVestor app launches.
  • Feedback: your message and, only if you add it, your email address, used to read your feedback and, if you want, to answer you.
  • With both forms we also receive whether you came from the website or the demo and your browser's language.

Please don't include sensitive personal information in your feedback.

4. Legal Basis

We process waitlist and feedback data based on your consent, which you give by submitting the form. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to salvador.bras.ferreira@gmail.com, and we will delete your data.

5. Service Providers

We do not sell or share your personal data. We use these providers to run the website and the demo:

  • Formspree: receives the waitlist and feedback forms and forwards them to us by email. Formspree is based in the United States.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and the demo. Like any web host, it processes your IP address to deliver the pages.
  • Google: fonts, and the graphics engine the demo needs to run, are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs): serves the icons on the home page and receives your IP address.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Cookies and Analytics

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The demo uses your browser's storage only to keep your demo progress on your device.

7. Data Retention

  • Waitlist: until the app launches and we have told you about it, or until you ask us to remove you.
  • Feedback: as long as needed to handle it, and no longer than two years.
  • Demo data: stays on your device until you reset the demo or clear your browser data.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise them, write to salvador.bras.ferreira@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (www.cnpd.pt).

9. Children

LearnVestor is intended for people aged 16 or older. Please don't use the forms if you are under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will point out important changes on this website.
